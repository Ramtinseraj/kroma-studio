# KROMA STUDIO — Next-Gen Pro Creative Engine

## North Star: "Cyber-Obsidian & Neon Glass"
High-tech, futuristic dark studio interface designed for high-performance mobile, tablet, and desktop creators. Dark obsidian surfaces with vibrant neon cyan (`#00f0ff`), sky blue (`#38bdf8`), ultraviolet (`#a855f7`), pink (`#f43f5e`), and emerald (`#10b981`) accents. Glassmorphic capsule cards with dedicated purpose-built vector artworks and crisp tool glyphs.

## Screen Real Estate & Viewport Fit
- **Zero Bottom Bars:** All floating and overlapping bottom taskbars have been removed so the 6 core capsules own 100% of the screen height.
- **Header Actions:** Quick-access tools (`Creator Profile`, `Cloud Sync Vault`, and `Project History`) are neatly docked in the top header.
- **100% Viewport Fit:** Designed with `h-[100dvh]` and responsive flex distribution so all 6 capsules are fully visible on any phone or tablet without vertical scrolling.

## 6 Main Capsules: Purpose-Driven Artworks & Glyphs
1. **VIDEO LAB**
   - **Create Video (Capsule 1):**
     - *Function:* 4K 60FPS Video Shooting & Rig Recording.
     - *Background Artwork:* Cine Rig Camera with Matte Box, optical lens elements, dual rods, and 16:9 frame guide.
     - *Logo:* Movie Camcorder (`videocam`).
   - **Video Archive (Capsule 2):**
     - *Function:* Timeline Editing & Project Stems Vault.
     - *Background Artwork:* Multi-track NLE Video Editing Timeline with filmstrip clips, audio waveform track, time ruler, and red playhead scrubber.
     - *Logo:* Video Timeline Editor (`movie_edit`).

2. **PHOTO HUB**
   - **Create Photo (Capsule 3):**
     - *Function:* RAW HDR Capture & 3D Optics.
     - *Background Artwork:* Professional SLR Camera with 8-blade aperture iris diaphragm, 3x3 rule-of-thirds grid reticle, and autofocus target box.
     - *Logo:* Photo Camera (`photo_camera`).
   - **Photo Archive (Capsule 4):**
     - *Function:* Photo Gallery & Asset Stems.
     - *Background Artwork:* Layered Photo Gallery Album Stack with landscape photographs, corner tabs, and picture cards.
     - *Logo:* Photo Gallery Stack (`collections`).

3. **AUDIO & AI LAB**
   - **Audio Studio & FX (Capsule 5):**
     - *Function:* Hi-Res Voice Recording & Sound Effects.
     - *Background Artwork:* Broadcast Dynamic Studio Microphone with shockmount suspension and 12-band graphic equalizer frequency bars.
     - *Logo:* Broadcast Microphone (`mic`).
   - **AI Assistant (Capsule 6):**
     - *Function:* Generative AI Neural Copilot & Prompt Engine.
     - *Background Artwork:* Futuristic Cybernetic Neural Brain with interconnected synapses, glowing data nodes, and AI sparkle geometry.
     - *Logo:* AI Neural Copilot (`psychology`).
